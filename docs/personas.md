# Personas

**Proto-personas.** These are built from the client's answers (families with
children aged 10–14 are the core audience, Q8; visitors and members are the
primary users, Q18; staff publish events and update hours, Q4) and the
team's knowledge of regional museums — not from interviews. Usability
testing (`usability-testing.md`) recruits participants matching them so the
assumptions get tested.

Each persona lists the requirements it drives (`requirements.md`).

---

## 1. Sarah Nguyen — the planning parent *(primary)*

> "If I can't work out what's on and what it costs in two minutes, we'll go to the beach instead."

| | |
|---|---|
| Who | 41, Adelaide Hills, two children (Mia 11, Leo 13), works part-time |
| Device | Phone, usually in the evening or in the car park |
| Goals | Find something both kids will enjoy; know the cost and times before committing; avoid queues |
| Frustrations | Information only on posters; having to phone to check opening hours; booking forms that ask for everything twice |
| Needs from us | Hours and "open now" on the home page; family-suitable labels; prices in plain dollars; booking in a minute on a phone; a pass she can show on arrival |
| Drives | FR-01, FR-06, FR-16, FR-17, NFR-03, NFR-07 |

**Scenario.** On Thursday night Sarah opens the site from a school-group
message, sees the museum is open Saturday 10–4, finds the "Junior Field
Detective" workshop labelled *Ages 10–14*, books two adults and two children
for the 10:00 window, ticks *sensory backpack* for Leo, and keeps the pass
on her phone.
